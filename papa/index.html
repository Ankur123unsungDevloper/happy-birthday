To My Papa

Dear Papa,

I may not always say it, and I may not always know how to express it, but I want you to know how grateful I am to have you in my life.

You have always been more than just my father. You have been my guide when I was lost, my strength when things were difficult, and the person who quietly stood behind me even when I didn’t realize it.

Everything I am becoming carries a little piece of what you have taught me.

Thank you for every sacrifice, every lesson, every piece of advice, and every moment you’ve been there for me.

Today, I just want to say…

Thank you, Papa.
I love you. ❤
